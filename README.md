Mathematics undergraduate at [USP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_S%C3%A3o_Paulo), interested in **mathematical modeling, statistical inference, scientific computing, systems, and reverse engineering**.

Most of my reverse-engineering work is around networks: undocumented protocols, traffic analysis, and client-server behavior. I also work with binaries, game internals, anti-cheat systems, and software protection. Much of this work grew through [Dark Seal](https://github.com/darkseal-org), a reverse-engineering and software research collective focused largely on the League of Legends ecosystem.

My engineering background includes ETL architectures, statistical pipelines, distributed services, and high-concurrency systems. I have also done security research and responsible disclosure through [HackerOne](https://www.hackerone.com/), involving Amazon, Riot Games, and Mercado Livre.

I like building projects from scratch and leaving them open. I believe knowledge should be free, and that code, documentation, and research become more useful when other people can inspect them and continue from where you stopped.

There are two lines I keep coming back to:

> *After we leave, they will build schools and hospitals for you, and increase your wages.*
> *This is not because they have a conscience, nor because they have become good people, but because we have been here.*

Outside research, I do competitive programming through [GEMA](https://www.icmc.usp.br/cultura-e-extensao/grupos-de-extensao) and the [SBC Programming Contest](https://maratona.sbc.org.br/), read Drummond and Andy Weir, and keep finding new uses for Raspberry Pis.
